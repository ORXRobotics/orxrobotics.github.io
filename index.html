Precision. Brevity. Transparency.

Welcome to ORX, a community dedicated to a single mission: proving that elite-level engineering does not require an elite-level budget. The rising costs of traditional robotics competitions are becoming unbearable for teams. We decided to build something different—a league where creativity and strategic logic matter more than a team’s bank account.

As a member of the HQ team, you will be the one that teams rely on. You will have access to our shared repositories and design folders to iterate rapidly, keeping Engineering Vitality as our core focus.

Active Sectors of Operation

CAD Modelling & Mechanical Design
Develop 3D models for field elements and official kit parts. Prioritize durability, low-cost manufacturing, and assembly with minimal tools.
Software Development
Write and optimize libraries for our ESP-32 based control system. Includes firmware, API documentation, Field Management System (FMS), and driver-stations.
Electrical Engineering
Design and test power distribution and sensor integration for core kits and main boards.
Technical Documentation
Author manuals and assembly guides. Requires a clear, instructional, no-fluff tone that gets straight to the point.
Communications & Advertising
Execute brand guidelines across digital and print media to recruit teams and sponsors.
