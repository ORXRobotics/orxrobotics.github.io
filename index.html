ORX HQ

We are proving that elite-level engineering does not require an elite-level budget. Traditional robotics competition costs are becoming unbearable for schools and independent teams alike. We are engineering a new architectural standard to run competitive tournaments that are structurally more affordable than anything else currently operating.

The Paradigm Shift

The Current Burden

Proprietary control ecosystems, vendor lock-in, and ballooning metal kit prices restrict high-tier technical exposure to well-funded environments.

The Open Solution

An open-source standard built around baseline manufacturing: standard stock components, distributed standard electronics, and crowdsourced CAD hardware files.

To scale this framework into reality for the upcoming season, ORX HQ functions as an open assembly line. We require developers, operators, and mechanics to finalize the core ecosystem layout. Accepted volunteers will be granted entry to core workspaces to execute rapid deployment.

Active Development Sectors

CAD & Open-Source Part Libraries

Modeling reliable, accessible structural parts for game fields and primary kits. Focus areas: extreme durability profiles, standard raw stock alignment, and rapid low-tool manufacturing. Accepted builders receive repository read/write clearance for direct commits and pull request iterations.

💡 Prefer standard file sharing? Skip git overhead and email your `.STEP` or project folders straight to our engineering deck.

Software Infrastructure

Writing custom, low-overhead firmware and libraries for our ESP-32 based hardware stack. Includes development on localized field management software (FMS) and driver-station communication layers.

Electrical Engineering

Validating schematic routing, stress-testing power distribution architectures, and standardizing sensor integration brackets across entry-level modular electronics boards.

Technical Documentation

Drafting assembly specifications, system wiring charts, and layout regulations. Objective clarity over stylistic filler.

Ecosystem Communications

Managing brand assets, open asset delivery frameworks, and community outreach channels to cleanly onboard structural groups and team leagues.
